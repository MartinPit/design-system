# Reference cards

The originating image exists only in the chat that started this effort. This transcription is the canonical source for every ticket. Two portrait cards sit side by side, with a hand-drawn squiggle arrow pointing from the left card to the right.

## Card A — the seed for Arcana

Portrait card, roughly 3:4, generous cream ground (`#F6F1DF`–`#FAF6E8`), slightly rounded corners, flat and printed rather than glowing.

**Upper two-thirds — the gradient form.** A single large organic shape, like a petal or a leaf lying on its side:
- Left edge vertical, flush-ish with the text margin.
- Top edge runs flat then breaks into a wide arc sweeping down to the right.
- Bottom edge bows down into a deep scoop, ending in a point at the lower left.
- Fill is a smooth multi-stop gradient: amber/orange at upper left, hot magenta and violet at lower left, crimson/deep rose at the right. No texture, no highlights — pure flat gradient.

**Middle — identity line.** A deep purple monogram `B` (heavy, geometric sans, `#4A0D63`-ish), followed by five pale lavender squares in a row (a redacted/placeholder strip), then the words `GIFTED MAGICIAN` in purple, uppercase, letterspaced, small.

**Bottom row.**
- Two circular line-art seal badges in blue/violet: `ARC` arcana-style marks, one numbered `1`, one numbered `3`, with tiny surrounding text.
- A boxed strip reading `ARC` | `Jul 13, 2024`, thin purple keyline, small uppercase type.
- Right side: `BROWSER` / `COMPANY` stacked in purple, uppercase, letterspaced.

**Read:** an institutional archive or membership card. Swiss/editorial grid, flat print, high-contrast ink on cream, one flamboyant gradient allowed to carry all the colour. Cold purple typography against warm accents. The vibe is *archive, membership, collection, provenance*.

## Card B — the seed for Tidal

Portrait card, roughly 3:4, fully rounded (superellipse) corners.

**Surface.** A dark-to-light iridescent gradient: deep indigo at lower left, through violet and periwinkle, into cyan and mint at the upper right. It reads like light through water, not like a flat fill.

**Pattern.** The whole card is covered in a fine `seigaiha` (青海波) scale pattern — overlapping concentric arcs, drawn as thin light lines at low opacity, denser than a texture, quieter than an illustration. Conforms to the card, not to a rectangle.

**Top left.** A black spiral / nautilus shell logomark on a small light disc.

**Lower left.**
- `Bard` in a high-contrast display serif, black, large. Didone-adjacent, with fine hairlines.
- `Member Since` in tiny letterspaced caps above `2026.11` in a small serif/mono numeric style.

**Lower right.** A cluster of translucent glass bubbles or a debossed orb, with specular highlights and a soft inner glow; it catches the iridescent light rather than sitting on top of it.

**Read:** aquatic, celestial, premium fintech. Ornamental but restrained by one repeating geometry. Serif authority against a liquid, luminous ground. The vibe is *depth, membership, rarity, a token you hold*.

## How the two differ

| | Card A / Arcana | Card B / Tidal |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | warm cream, opaque, printed | dark indigo→mint, luminous, layered |
| Colour use | ink + one gradient event | gradient everywhere + thin light linework |
| Type | geometric/neo-grotesque sans, uppercase labels | high-contrast display serif + tiny caps |
| Shape | sharp grid, asymmetric organic blob | superellipse, repeating arc geometry |
| Depth | flat, keylines, seals | glass, glow, specular highlight |
| Feeling | institutional archive | liquid heirloom |

Both are membership artifacts — cards, tiers, "member since", archives. That is the bridge to a flashcards app: decks as collections, study history as provenance, recall as membership in a body of knowledge.
